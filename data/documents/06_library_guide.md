# Central Library Guide

**Aravali Institute of Technology (AIT)** — Central Library

*This is a fictional document written for a demo project. Numbers are invented.*

## About

The Central Library gives students, faculty and researchers access to printed books, e-books, e-journals, video lectures, project reports and competitive-exam books. It follows an open-access system: users can walk into the stack area to browse and pick books. Books are arranged by the Dewey Decimal Classification.

## Timings

- **Central Library:** 9:00 am to 6:00 pm on all working days.
- **Reading Hall:** 6:00 am to 11:30 pm on all days, including holidays and exam periods. It seats 250 students and has Wi-Fi and charging points. Entry is through a barcode register.

## Sections

| Section | What it holds |
|---|---|
| Stack area | Subject-wise book collection; location charts help find books |
| PG section | Textbooks and reference books for M.Tech courses, with seating |
| Reference section (mezzanine floor) | Reference books for the syllabus. Read in the library only |
| Periodical section | Latest magazines and journals, with back issues |
| Reading hall | Self-study space |
| Group study area | Discussion with friends or teachers |
| Property counter | Bags, helmets and belongings must be kept here before entering |

## Borrowing rules

| Member | Books at a time | Loan period |
|---|---|---|
| UG student | 4 | 14 days |
| PG student | 6 | 21 days |
| Faculty | 10 | 90 days |

- Renewal: up to 2 times if no one has reserved the book.
- Late fine: **Rs. 2 per book per day**.
- Lost book: replace it with the same edition or pay twice the price.
- Reference-section books and periodicals cannot be issued.
- Library membership is free and starts when the student's admission is confirmed. A **no-dues certificate** from the library is required at graduation.

## Digital library

- One single login gives access to e-journals, e-books and the national digital library.
- Off-campus access is available through the institute VPN.
- For help with e-resources, national digital library enrolment, mini-projects and internship reports, email **library@ait.example.edu**.

## Other services

- Book-drop box for returns after hours
- Training sessions on literature search and publication ethics
- Plagiarism check support for project reports and papers (on request)
- Book recommendation requests through the library portal
