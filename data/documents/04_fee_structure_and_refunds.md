## Due dates and payment

- Fees for each academic year are due by **31 August**.
- Pay through the accounts portal (UPI, net banking, card) or by demand draft in favour of the institute. Cash is not accepted.
- Part payment: the accounts office may allow payment in **two instalments** if requested in writing before the due date. The second instalment is due within 40 days.
- A **late fee of Rs. 100 per day** (maximum Rs. 5,000) applies after the due date.
- Receipts are available on the portal under "Fee History".

## What happens if fees are not paid

1. **Overdue fee notice** from the accounts office.
2. After the last date announced in the notice, the student **cannot collect the hall ticket** for the semester examinations and cannot register for the next semester.
3. Grade cards and certificates are not released while dues are pending.
4. Students with overdue fees are **not eligible for institute grants or scholarships**.

## Refund policy for withdrawal

| When the student withdraws | Refund |
|---|---|
| Before the commencement of classes | Full fee minus Rs. 5,000 processing charge |
| Within 15 days after classes begin | 80% of the tuition and development fee |
| Between 16 and 30 days after classes begin | 50% of the tuition and development fee |
| After 30 days | No refund |

- The **caution deposit** is refunded in full after a no-dues clearance, on leaving the institute.
- Management and NRI quota fees follow the same refund slabs.
- Refunds are paid by bank transfer within 30 working days of the application.
- Students who take admission in another college through a centralised round and cancel here before the last date of the round get the "before classes" refund.
