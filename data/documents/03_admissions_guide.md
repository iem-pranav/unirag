## Eligibility

**B.Tech (first year)**
- Passed 12th standard (or equivalent) with Physics and Mathematics, plus Chemistry, Biology, Computer Science or Technical Vocational subject.
- At least **50%** aggregate in the above subjects (**45%** for reserved categories).
- A valid score in the State Common Entrance Test (CET) or JEE Main.

**Direct Second Year B.Tech**
- 3-year diploma in Engineering with at least 50% (45% reserved), or B.Sc. with Mathematics.

**M.Tech**
- B.Tech/B.E. in a relevant branch with at least 50% (45% reserved) and a valid GATE score, or the institute entrance test.

## How admission works

1. **Register** on the state admission portal and upload documents.
2. **Document verification** at a facilitation centre.
3. **Merit list** is published by the state authority using CET/JEE Main scores.
4. **Option form:** choose colleges and branches in order of preference.
5. **Seat allotment** over centralised rounds. A student can accept, freeze or float the seat.
6. **Report to AIT** within the stated days, pay the fees, and submit original documents.

Institute-level, management and NRI quota seats are filled by the institute through its own merit list after the centralised rounds.
