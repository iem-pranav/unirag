## How to read these numbers

- The **median** is the number to plan around. The mean is pulled up by a few very high packages.
- Offers are counted per offer, not per student. A few students may hold more than one offer under the placement policy.
- A company that visits more than once in a year appears as separate drives.
- For exact company-wise numbers and branch splits, see the placement tables.
