# Institute-Industry Partnership Cell (IIPC)

**Aravali Institute of Technology (AIT)**

*This is a fictional document written for a demo project. Numbers are invented.*

## About AIT

Aravali Institute of Technology was established in 1985 as an autonomous institute affiliated to Western Hills University. It offers B.Tech programmes in Computer Engineering, Information Technology, and Electronics and Telecommunication, and M.Tech programmes in Computer Engineering and Data Science. The institute is a recognised research centre of the university in Computer Engineering and Electronics and Telecommunication. As an autonomous institute, AIT can update its curriculum quickly to match industry needs.

## What the IIPC does

The IIPC connects AIT with companies so students and faculty get real-world exposure. Industry can work with AIT in these ways:

- **Joint events:** workshops, conferences and symposia.
- **Faculty-industry exchange:** industrial visits, expert lectures and knowledge sharing.
- **Industry-linked student projects:** UG and PG projects guided jointly by faculty and industry mentors.
- **Training programmes:** faculty-led training for working engineers.
- **Curriculum participation:** industry experts in curriculum design and review.
- **MoUs:** formal agreements for strategic and operational links.
- **Student scholarships and fellowships:** financial support from industry.
- **Internship programmes:** structured internships with partner companies.
- **Consultancy:** faculty expertise for industry problems.
- **Joint research and field studies.**
- **Short-term faculty assignments in industry.**
- **Industry-sponsored R&D labs and centres of excellence.**
- **Professorial chairs** sponsored by industry.

## How students can use the IIPC

- Ask for **internship leads** through the IIPC portal. Internships in the winter and summer windows can be approved as credit-bearing internship courses (see the Academic Regulations).
- Propose an **industry-linked project** with a faculty mentor.
- Attend **expert lectures and industrial visits**. Visits count towards activity points.
