# Extra-Curricular Activities

**Aravali Institute of Technology (AIT)** — Student Activities Office

*This is a fictional document written for a demo project.*

## Cultural Club

Supports students in taking their ideas to inter-college cultural events by giving social, technical, environmental and financial help. Two active groups:
- **Art Circle:** painting, sketching, theatre and set design.
- **Debate Society:** debates, group discussions and public speaking.

## Sports and Physical Education

Facilities: basketball, volleyball, table tennis, chess, carrom and a gym. Students compete against other colleges and universities. The sports office also helps students manage teams and plan events. Sports achievements can earn the **Sports and Culture Excellence Award** (see Scholarships and Financial Aid).

## Campus magazine: Prism

A non-technical club that publishes the college magazine. It has published 27 volumes so far. Students can join as writers, designers, photographers or editors.

## IdeaTalks

An independent talk event where speakers share ideas, stories and innovations. A student team handles speakers, venue, design and sponsorship.

## Model United Nations (MUN) Club

Started in 2016. Students discuss social, political and economic issues, and the club motto is "Think. Discuss. Prosper." It runs an annual MUN conference, Intra-MUN, open mic, extempore, group discussion and debate events, and publishes a monthly newsletter written by students.

## Career Guidance Cell

Run with the state higher-education department. It guides students on careers, competitive exams (civil services, banking, defence, police, insurance), entrepreneurship, skill development, internships and placements.

- **Who can join:** any regular student of the institute, or an alumnus referred by the coordinator.
- **Registration fee:** Rs. 365, one time, valid for the three-year degree period. It is returned as a subsidy on credit-course exam charges.
- **How to register:** download the state skill-development app and pay the fee online.
- **Contact:** the college coordinator in the Student Activities Office.

## NSS and social service

NSS volunteers run a blood-donation camp every August, a festival-time eco-friendly idol workshop, and village visits. Volunteering earns activity points (see Co-Curricular Activities).

## Joining and funding

Clubs can request funding from the Student Activities Office by submitting an event plan and budget at least two weeks before the event.
