# Student Research and Project Grants Handbook, 2026-27

**Aravali Institute of Technology (AIT)** — R&D Cell

*This is a fictional document written for a demo project.*

This handbook lists the grants a student can apply for, **who is eligible**, and how to apply. **Closing dates and result dates are not printed here.** They change every cycle and are published in the grants calendar on the R&D portal.

## Rules that apply to every grant

1. **Year of study** means the year the student is currently in: 1 = first year (FE), 2 = second year (SE), 3 = third year (TE), 4 = final year (BE).
2. **CGPA** is the CGPA on the latest issued grade card, that is, the last completed semester. The semester in progress does not count.
3. **Active backlogs** are courses with an F grade that are not yet cleared. Unless a grant says otherwise, a student must have **zero active backlogs**.
4. **Attendance:** the student must not be below 75% in any course of the semester in progress.
5. **Fees:** the student must have **no overdue fee entries**. A student with unpaid fees past the due date is not eligible, even if all other criteria are met.
6. **Discipline:** no disciplinary action against the student in the last 12 months.
7. **Research-type grants** (Undergraduate Research Seed Grant, AI and Data Research Fellowship, Women in Tech Research Grant, Embedded and Wireless Research Grant): a student can hold **only one** at a time.
8. A student's total support from all grants in one academic year is capped at **Rs. 1,25,000**.
9. A **faculty guide** of the student's own department (or an approved guide from another department) must sign the application.
10. Late applications are not accepted. Incomplete applications are rejected without notice.

## The grants

### 1. Undergraduate Research Seed Grant (up to Rs. 50,000)
Funds a small research study for six months.
- Eligible: students in **year 3 or year 4**, from **any branch**.
- CGPA of **8.0 or above**.
- Zero active backlogs.
- A proposal of up to 5 pages and a faculty guide.
- Obligation: a final report and a poster at the annual research day.

### 2. Student Innovation Project Grant (up to Rs. 75,000)
Funds a team building a working prototype.
- Team of **2 to 4** students; at least one member must be in year 2, 3 or 4, and every member must have a CGPA of **7.0 or above**. The team lead must have zero active backlogs.
- Any branch. A faculty mentor is required.
- Obligation: a prototype demo within six months and a short video.

### 3. Conference Travel Grant (up to Rs. 40,000)
Pays registration and travel for presenting a paper.
- Eligible: students in **year 3 or year 4** and M.Tech students.
- CGPA of **7.5 or above**.
- The paper must be **accepted** at an indexed conference, with the student as **first author**.
- Up to Rs. 15,000 for a domestic conference and up to Rs. 40,000 for an international one.
- Only **one travel grant per student per academic year**.
- Apply at least **21 days before** the travel date. Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis.

### 4. AI and Data Research Fellowship (Rs. 1,20,000, paid as Rs. 20,000 a month for six months)
Sponsored by an industry partner for work in machine learning and data.
- Eligible: **Computer Engineering and Information Technology** students only, in **year 3 or year 4**.
- CGPA of **8.5 or above**.
- Zero active backlogs.
- The student must have **completed the core course of their branch** (**Machine Learning** for CE students, **Data Science** for IT students) with grade **A or above**, **or** be **currently enrolled** in that course and have an **ISE score of at least 12 out of 15** in it.
- Selection includes a technical interview with the sponsor.

### 5. Women in Tech Research Grant (up to Rs. 60,000)
Supports women students in research and prototyping.
- Eligible: **women students** in **year 3 or year 4** from **any branch**.
- CGPA of **7.5 or above**.
- Zero active backlogs.
- Each grantee is paired with a woman mentor from the alumni network.

### 6. Embedded and Wireless Research Grant (up to Rs. 60,000)
For hardware-oriented research.
- Eligible: **Electronics and Telecommunication** students only, in **year 3 or year 4**.
- CGPA of **7.5 or above**.
- Zero active backlogs.
- The proposal must include a hardware component (for example a sensor, radio or embedded board).

### 7. Final-Year Project Funding (up to Rs. 30,000)
Helps with components, cloud credits and testing costs.
- Eligible: **year 4 students only**, any branch.
- CGPA of **6.5 or above**.
- A student may have **one** active backlog.
- The project title must be approved by the department project committee.

### 8. Summer Research Internship Stipend (Rs. 25,000)
Supports an 8-week research internship in an AIT lab or at a partner organisation in the summer.
- Eligible: students in **year 2 or year 3**, any branch.
- CGPA of **8.0 or above**.
- Overall attendance of **80% or more** in the last completed semester.
- Zero active backlogs.
- An acceptance letter or a lab supervisor's confirmation is required.

## How to apply

1. Log in to the R&D portal and open the grants calendar to check that the call is open.
2. Fill in the application form and upload the proposal.
3. Upload the faculty guide's signed recommendation.
4. The portal reads your CGPA, backlogs and fee status automatically. You cannot edit them.
5. Submit before the closing date.

## Selection

The R&D committee scores each proposal on:
- Relevance and originality: 35%
- Feasibility and plan: 25%
- Student's academic record: 20%
- Guide's recommendation: 20%

Shortlisted students may be asked for a 10-minute presentation. Results are published on the portal and by email.

## Payment

Funds are paid in two parts: 60% on approval and 40% after the final report is accepted. The fellowship in grant 4 and the stipend in grant 8 are paid monthly. Receipts must be submitted for all expenses.
