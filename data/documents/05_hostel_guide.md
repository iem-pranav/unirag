# Hostel Guide

**Aravali Institute of Technology (AIT)** — Hostel Office

*This is a fictional document written for a demo project.*

## Capacity

| Hostel | Rooms | Occupancy |
|---|---|---|
| Girls hostel | 100 | double occupancy, attached bathroom |
| Boys hostel | 80 | double occupancy, attached bathroom |

Seats are allotted by year of study. Priority goes to first-year students and students admitted through the centralised rounds from outside the city. Remaining seats go to second, third and fourth-year students by merit and distance from home.

| Year | Girls hostel seats | Boys hostel seats |
|---|---|---|
| First year and Direct Second Year | 80 | 70 |
| Second year | 50 | 40 |
| Third year | 40 | 30 |
| Final year | 30 | 20 |

## Charges (per academic year)

| Item | Amount (Rs.) |
|---|---|
| Room rent, double occupancy with attached bathroom | 1,20,000 |
| Refundable hostel deposit (one time) | 12,000 |
| Mess charges | paid monthly to the mess contractor, about Rs. 3,800 |

Hostel fees are paid together with the institute fees by **31 August**. They appear in the fee ledger as "hostel fee".

## Facilities

- 24-hour hot water, Wi-Fi, laundry area, common room
- Hostel mess and a canteen
- Indoor games, outdoor courts and a gym
- Visiting medical officer; the campus health centre is open round the clock
- Two-wheeler parking and generator backup
- A lift in the girls hostel

## Rules

1. Entry timing: the hostel gate closes at **10:30 pm** for boys and **9:30 pm** for girls. Late entry needs a gate pass signed by the warden.
2. Visitors are allowed only in the visitors' room, between 5 pm and 7 pm.
3. Ragging is a criminal offence. Any incident should be reported to the warden or the anti-ragging committee.
4. Electrical appliances such as heaters and induction plates are not allowed.
5. Alcohol, tobacco and drugs are banned.
6. Students are responsible for damage to room property.
7. Night out needs a written parent permission and warden approval.
8. Rooms are inspected once a month.

## Leaving the hostel and deposit refund

Give one month's notice. The deposit is refunded after a no-dues check of the room and the mess. If a student leaves in the middle of the year, the room rent is refunded only as per the refund slabs in the **Fee Structure and Refund Policy**.
