# Co-Curricular Activities

**Aravali Institute of Technology (AIT)** — Student Activities Office

*This is a fictional document written for a demo project.*

Co-curricular activities are technical and professional activities connected to the curriculum. Students are encouraged to join at least one club every year.

## Activity points (mandatory audit course)

Every B.Tech student must collect **40 activity points** before the start of the final year. The course is graded PP/NP and does not affect CGPA.

| Activity | Points |
|---|---|
| Participation in a hackathon or technical contest | 5 |
| Winning or placing in a national-level contest | 15 |
| Paper presented at a conference | 10 |
| Organising a club event (core team) | 8 |
| Workshop or short course (certificate) | 4 |
| Volunteering in an institute fest | 3 |
| Open-source contribution (accepted pull requests, verified by faculty) | 5 |

Points are logged on the student activity portal with a certificate or mentor verification.

## Attendance for activities

Students on approved institute duty get attendance waiver for the event days only (see the Academic Regulations). Ask your HOD for approval **before** the event. Practice sessions are not covered.

## Clubs and events

### TechSpark: AIT International Hackathon
An annual 36-hour hackathon open to students from India and abroad. It is held in February. Teams of 3 to 4 build solutions for industry and social problems. AIT students can join as participants or as organisers.

### National hackathon participation
AIT sends teams to the national-level software hackathon run by the education ministry and partners. Internal selection rounds are held in the first semester. A team has 6 members; a faculty mentor is compulsory.

### AIT Computing Society
A student chapter of a professional computing society, started in 2012, with about 220 members. It runs coding contests, talks and mentoring. It has won a "best student chapter" award in the country twice.

### IEEE Student Branch
Established in 1990 to build technical awareness among members through talks, workshops and paper-writing sessions.

### IT Students' Society
Started in 2017 to help students grow in the IT industry through research, skill sharing and career guidance.

### Impulse: annual technical fest
The flagship technical event of AIT, with contests, project exhibitions and workshops.

### Robotics Team
Students build robots and compete in national robotics contests. The team meets over 90 colleges at the national event and explores real-world automation.

### Game Development Club
Started in 2021 with three students, and now has over 90 members. It runs workshops on game programming, design and visual art, and has taken part in game jams hosted by other colleges. The club plans to explore blockchain-based games.

### AIT Automobile Club
Students design, build and race electric vehicles. Electronics, mechanical, coding and management teams work together from a 3D model to a car on the track.

## How to join

Join through the student activities portal or meet the club coordinators at the clubs' fair held in the first month of every academic year. Membership is free.
