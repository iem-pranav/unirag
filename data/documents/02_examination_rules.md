# Examination Rules and Conduct

**Aravali Institute of Technology (AIT)** — Board of Examinations (BOE)

*This is a fictional document written for a demo project.*

## 1. Who runs examinations

- The **Controller of Examinations (CoE)** runs all examinations and signs the grade sheets.
- The **Board of Examinations (BOE)** frames the rules.
- The **Complaint Redressal Committee (CRC)** decides cases of unfair means. Its decision is final.

## 2. Hall ticket

- A hall ticket is generated for each student who meets the attendance rule (75%, or 65% with Principal's condonation).
- **No hall ticket is issued** if the student is detained for attendance, or has **tuition or development fees overdue** after the last date announced by the accounts office. Clear the dues first, then collect the hall ticket from the examination cell.
- Bring the hall ticket and the institute photo ID card to every paper. If the ID card is forgotten, a driving licence or another government photo ID is accepted after verification by the Senior Supervisor.

## 3. Timing and conduct in the exam hall

| Rule | ISE (in-semester) | ESE (end-semester) |
|---|---|---|
| Reach the exam block before start | 25 minutes | 25 minutes |
| Entry to the hall allowed from | 15 minutes before start | 15 minutes before start |
| Latest entry after start | 15 minutes | 30 minutes |
| Earliest exit after start | 30 minutes | 45 minutes |
| Leaving in the last 10 minutes | not allowed | not allowed |

- Wear simple, light clothes without large pockets. Slippers or simple sandals are preferred.
- Mobile phones, smart watches, calculators (unless allowed in the paper) and notes must be left at the property counter. Having them in the hall is treated as unfair means.
- Students are responsible for knowing their exam date, time and room.
- **Extra time:** differently abled students get 20 extra minutes per hour on a civil-surgeon certificate. Blind students get one extra hour. A **writer** can be arranged; the writer fee is Rs. 200 per paper for UG and Rs. 250 for PG, and is not charged from blind candidates.

## 4. Unfair means and punishment

Examples: copying, carrying notes or devices, impersonation, passing answers, tampering with the answer book, misbehaving with invigilators.

Penalties decided by the CRC can include:
1. Cancellation of the paper in which the offence happened.
2. Cancellation of all papers of that semester if the offence is repeated or serious.
3. Debarring from future examinations for one or more semesters.
4. Reporting to the university.

The student gets a written notice and a chance to explain. If the student does not appear, the committee decides on the evidence available.

## 5. Evaluation of answer books

- ESE answer books are assessed centrally (Central Assessment Programme) with moderation by senior faculty.
- After results, an **open house** lets students see their ISE or ESE answer books under supervision.

## 6. Photocopy and revaluation

1. A student who is not satisfied after the open house may apply for **revaluation** within **5 working days** after the open house.
2. Fee: **Rs. 500 per paper**. A photocopy of the answer book costs **Rs. 300 per paper**.
3. An external evaluator, different from the first one, checks the paper.
4. The revaluation marks replace the original marks only if they differ from the original by **10% or more** of the paper's total marks. Otherwise the original marks stay.
5. Revaluation is not available for practical or oral exams.
6. Answer books are kept for **one year** after the result is declared and then shredded.

## 7. Grace marks (university ordinances)

- **Passing:** up to 2 marks for a head of up to 50 marks, 3 marks for 51-100, 4 for 101-150, and 5 for 151-200, to help a student pass that head. The total grace must not exceed 1% of the aggregate marks.
- **Higher class:** a student who passes everything without grace but is short of the next class by at most 1% of the aggregate (or 10 marks, whichever is less) can get the marks needed. Grace given for passing is marked with a $ sign on the statement of marks.

## 8. Practical and oral exams

Held in the last week of teaching or as per the calendar. An external examiner is appointed. Internal and external examiners each carry 50% weight. If a student is absent, the same rules as for theory exams apply.

## 9. Results

Results are normally declared about four weeks after the last ESE paper. The CoE signs the grade sheet. Corrections of name or ID errors go through the examination cell.
