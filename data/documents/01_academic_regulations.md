# Rules and Regulations for Academics and Evaluation

**Aravali Institute of Technology (AIT)** is an autonomous institute affiliated to Western Hills University (WHU). These rules apply to B.Tech and M.Tech students admitted from the 2024-25 academic year onwards.

*This is a fictional document written for a demo project.*

## 1. Definitions

- **Credit:** the weight of a course. A normal semester carries 20 credits.
- **CIE:** Continuous Internal Evaluation (marks earned during the semester).
- **ISE:** In-Semester Examination, held centrally in the middle of the semester.
- **ESE:** End-Semester Examination, held at the end of the semester.
- **SGPA / CGPA:** Semester / Cumulative Grade Point Average, on a 10-point scale.
- **ATKT:** Allowed To Keep Term. It lets a student move to a higher semester with some credits pending.
- **Backlog:** a course in which the student has a final grade of F and has not yet cleared it.

## 2. Attendance rule

1. Every student must attend at least **75%** of the lectures, tutorials and labs of **each** registered course.
2. Students representing the institute in approved sports, cultural or technical events get attendance waiver for the event days only. Practice sessions do not count. Prior approval from the HOD is needed.
3. A student below 75% in a course is **detained** (grade DR) in that course, cannot take its ESE, and gets no hall ticket for it.
4. The Principal may **condone a shortage of up to 10 percentage points** (so a minimum of 65%) on genuine medical or family emergencies, on the HOD's recommendation and with documents. Condonation is not a right.
5. Departments send the list of defaulters to the examination cell 10 days before the ESE.
6. Attendance is recorded on the institute portal each day and locked at the end of every week.

### Withdrawal
A student may withdraw for up to one year for prolonged illness or a serious family problem. Apply within 15 days of the semester start with documents and a parent's endorsement. The degree must still be finished within 8 years of admission. There must be no dues pending with the institute, library or hostel.

## 3. Evaluation scheme

### Theory courses (UG), 100 marks
| Component | Marks | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Attendance | 5 | calculated from the portal |
| Internal assessments (IE1 + IE2) | 20 | assignments, quizzes, case studies, presentations |
| In-Semester Exam (ISE) | 15 | central exam, based on Module 1 |
| End-Semester Exam (ESE) | 60 | central exam, based on Modules 2, 3 and 4 |

### Practical and term-work courses
50 marks term work (experiments, journal, viva) + 50 marks practical or oral exam with an external examiner. Every experiment and the journal must be completed to get term work. An incomplete journal means the student is marked absent and must re-register.

### Other courses
- **Mini project, internship, seminar:** rubric-based reviews plus a report and viva.
- **MOOC courses:** assessed on assignments, performance and the certificate. See the credit cap below.
- **Life-skill / audit courses:** graded PP (pass) or NP (not passed). Passing is compulsory for the degree. Maximum two attempts.

## 4. Passing rules

- A student must score at least **40% of the total (CIE + ESE)** and at least **35% in the ESE** to pass a theory course.
- Term work, practical and oral heads need at least **40%** each.
- Credits are earned only when the course is passed.

### Re-examination
- Students with grade F in a theory course may take a **re-examination** after the ESE results.
- The re-exam fee is **Rs. 1,000 per theory course**. There is no re-exam for practical courses.
- Marks scored in the re-exam are counted at **90%** for grading.
- A student who fails the re-exam must **re-register** for the course in the next semester of the same type (odd or even), pay the fee, attend classes and appear for CIE and ESE again.
- A student absent from the ESE with a valid, pre-approved reason (hospitalisation, death of a close family member, approved institute representation) may take a **Re-ESE** without the 90% cap.
- Summer-term make-up classes may be offered by a department. A student can register for at most 4 courses (maximum 16 credits).

## 5. Grading

| Marks (%) | Grade | Grade point |
|---|---|---|
| 90 - 100 | O (Outstanding) | 10 |
| 80 - 89 | A+ | 9 |
| 70 - 79 | A | 8 |
| 60 - 69 | B+ | 7 |
| 55 - 59 | B | 6 |
| 50 - 54 | C | 5 |
| 40 - 49 | P (Pass) | 4 |
| below 40 | F (Fail) | 0 |
| absent | AB | 0 |

Special grades: **DR** (detained and repeat, due to attendance or incomplete term work), **WW** (willful withdrawal with satisfactory attendance), **IC** (incomplete due to illness, converted after the re-exam), **XX** (CIE of 80% or more but failed in ESE; can be used once in the whole programme for a theory course). IC and XX turn into F if not cleared.

### SGPA and CGPA
SGPA = (sum of grade point x credits of each course) / (total credits of the semester).
CGPA is calculated the same way over all semesters. Both are rounded to two decimals. When a failed course is passed later, the new grade replaces the old one.

### Class of degree
| CGPA | Class |
|---|---|
| 7.50 or more | First Class with Distinction |
| 6.50 to 7.49 | First Class |
| 6.00 to 6.49 | Higher Second Class |
| 5.25 to 5.99 | Second Class |
| 4.00 to 5.24 | Pass Class |

Percentage = CGPA x 9.5.

## 6. Moving to the next year (ATKT)

- To register for **Semester 3**, a student needs at least **55%** of the credits of Semesters 1 and 2 combined.
- To register for **Semester 5**, all credits of Semesters 1 and 2 and at least 55% of Semesters 3 and 4.
- To register for **Semester 7**, all credits of Semesters 3 and 4 and at least 55% of Semesters 5 and 6.
- Backlog Honors/Minors courses do not count for ATKT.

## 7. Award of degree

A B.Tech degree needs a minimum of **160 credits** (120 credits for lateral-entry students), a CGPA of at least **4.00**, all audit courses passed, all institute dues paid and no pending disciplinary case.

### Honors, Minors and MOOCs
- Honors/Minors are optional and can start from Semester 5. Their credits are extra and are **not** counted in SGPA or CGPA.
- Credits from approved MOOCs are capped at 8 per programme.

## 8. Discipline and conduct

Students must carry the institute ID card on campus. The following are violations: ragging (a criminal offence), damaging property, abusive behaviour, forgery, sharing IT login details, possessing alcohol or drugs on campus, and organising meetings without permission. Cases go to the Grievance Redressal and Discipline Committee (GRDC), appointed by the Principal.

## 9. Grade card

A grade card is issued after every semester. It shows courses, credits, letter grades, credits earned, SGPA, CGPA, and the grading scale.
