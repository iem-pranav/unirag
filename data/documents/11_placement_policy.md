# Training and Placement Policy

**Aravali Institute of Technology (AIT)** — Training and Placement Office (TPO)

*This is a fictional document written for a demo project.*

## Who can sit for placements

- Students in the **final year** (year 4, semesters 7 and 8) of B.Tech and the final year of M.Tech.
- Third-year students can take part in **internship drives** only.
- Students must register on the placement portal at the start of semester 7 and keep their profile and resume up to date.

## Eligibility for a drive

1. A minimum **CGPA of 6.00** on the latest grade card (a company can ask for more).
2. **Zero active backlogs** for Group I drives (see below). **One active backlog** is allowed for Group II drives.
3. No disciplinary action in the last 12 months.
4. No overdue fees.
5. The student must satisfy the branch and criteria stated by the company.

## Recruiter groups

- **Group I:** annual package of **5 LPA or more**.
- **Group II:** annual package **below 5 LPA**.

The package for grouping is the total fixed annual pay offered at the time of the drive. Bonuses and stock options are not counted.

## One-offer policy (to give every student a fair chance)

| Student status | Rules |
|---|---|
| No offer | Can sit for any drive for which they are eligible |
| Offer from a Group II company | Can sit for Group I drives |
| Offer from a Group I company | Cannot sit for more drives, **except** a drive marked "dream", where the package is at least **1.5 times** the current offer |

- An offer accepted through the TPO cannot be changed after the offer letter is signed.
- A student who **does not show up** for a drive after registering without a valid reason can be **debarred for one year**.
- A student who **backs out after accepting** an offer can be blacklisted for the rest of the placement season.

## Internships and pre-placement offers (PPO)

- A PPO from an internship is counted as a placement offer in the same group as its package.
- Internship stipends are not counted towards the package.
- Third-year students can take up internships in the winter and summer windows (see the academic calendar). Attendance rules still apply for the days in a semester.

## Training provided

- Aptitude and reasoning practice from semester 5
- Resume and LinkedIn workshops
- Mock interviews with alumni
- Coding practice sessions and a mock hackathon
- Soft-skill and group discussion practice

## Conduct

- Formal dress is compulsory for drives and interviews.
- Mobile phones are off during tests and interviews.
- Any form of cheating or misbehaviour leads to disqualification from the drive and further action by the disciplinary committee.

## Placement statistics

Yearly figures are published in the **Placement Highlights** document and the placement tables.
