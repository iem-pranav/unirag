# How to Read the Academic Calendar 2026-27

**Aravali Institute of Technology (AIT)** — Dean Academics

*This is a fictional document written for a demo project. The exact dates of every event are in the academic calendar table. This note explains how the semester is organised.*

## Year groups used in the calendar

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| FE | First year B.Tech |
| SE | Second year B.Tech (includes Direct Second Year students) |
| TE | Third year B.Tech |
| BE | Final year B.Tech |
| PG | M.Tech students |
| STAFF | Faculty and staff events |
| ALL | Everyone |

## Typical flow of an odd semester (Semester I of the year)

1. **Commencement of instruction.** Final-year students start first, then SE, TE and PG, and first-year students start in August.
2. **In-semester examination (ISE)** for 15 marks. Different year groups have their ISE in different weeks, so central rooms and evaluators can be shared.
3. **ISE assessment and marks entry**, then an **open house** where students see their answer books, and a result declaration.
4. **Mid-semester feedback** and later the **end-semester feedback and course exit survey**. Filling both is compulsory.
5. **Continuous internal evaluation (CIE) marks compilation** and **term-work compilation.** After this, internal marks are displayed to students.
6. **Conclusion of instruction.**
7. **Practical, oral, seminar and dissertation exams** with external examiners.
8. **End-semester examination (ESE)**, followed by **assessment** and an **open house** for answer books.
9. **ESE result declaration.**
10. **Winter vacation and internship window** is placed between the end of the ESE and the start of the next semester, and the next semester begins in January.

## Holidays and events

Public holidays are shown as "(holiday)". Classes may be held on a Saturday to compensate. Student club events such as the debate event, the entrepreneurship fest, the model UN conclave and the blood-donation camp are listed so departments avoid scheduling exams on those days.

## Things to remember

- Dates can change when the university changes its guidelines. The latest version is always on the institute portal.
- The ESE timetable (room and time) is published about two weeks before the first paper.
- Re-exam dates are announced after the ESE results.
- A student detained for attendance does not take the ESE (see Academic Regulations).
- Exam rules for reporting time, ID and hall ticket are in the Examination Rules.
