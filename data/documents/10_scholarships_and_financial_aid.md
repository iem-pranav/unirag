# Scholarships and Financial Aid 2026-27

**Aravali Institute of Technology (AIT)** — Accounts Office and Student Welfare Cell

*This is a fictional document written for a demo project. Amounts and conditions are invented.*

## Institute scholarships

### 1. AIT Merit Scholarship
- For students of **year 2, 3 and 4**.
- CGPA of **9.0 or above** on the latest grade card.
- The top **5 students of each branch** by CGPA get a **50% waiver of the tuition fee** for the next academic year.
- Renewal needs a CGPA of at least 8.5 and zero backlogs.
- No separate application. The Accounts Office selects students after the even-semester results.

### 2. Need-Based Support Fund
- For any year, if the **annual family income is below Rs. 4,00,000**.
- Covers up to **Rs. 60,000** of fees, paid directly to the account.
- Documents: income certificate, ration card or equivalent, last year's tax return (if any) and a short statement.
- **Apply by 15 September** each year at the Student Welfare Cell. Decisions come by 15 October.
- A student must keep at least 75% attendance and have no disciplinary case to receive the aid.

### 3. Sports and Culture Excellence Award
- For students who won a medal or position at the national level in sports, performing arts or an equivalent cultural contest during the previous year.
- **25% waiver of the tuition fee** for one year. Certificates must be signed by the sports or culture coordinator.
- Apply by 31 July each year.

### 4. Alumni Endowment Scholarship
- For **first-year students** who rank in the top 3 in the admission merit list of their branch.
- A one-time award of **Rs. 40,000**, funded by alumni.
- Selected automatically at the time of admission.

## Government schemes

Students from reserved categories and economically weaker sections can get fee waivers and scholarships through state and central government portals. The Student Welfare Cell helps with forms and document verification, but the amount and decision come from the government authority. Keep your caste and income certificates up to date.

## Rules common to institute scholarships

1. A student can receive **only one institute scholarship** in an academic year, the one with the higher value.
2. Scholarships are **adjusted against the tuition fee**. Hostel fees are not covered unless stated.
3. A student with **overdue fees**, an **active disciplinary case**, or **attendance below 75%** in the semester in progress is not eligible.
4. Scholarships can be **withdrawn** if false information is found.
5. Government scholarships can be combined with institute scholarships only if the government rules allow it.

## Research grants

Research and project grants (for example the Undergraduate Research Seed Grant) are different from scholarships. See the **Student Research and Project Grants Handbook**.
