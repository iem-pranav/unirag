# Campus IT and Network Services

**Aravali Institute of Technology (AIT)** — IT Services

*This is a fictional document written for a demo project. Numbers are invented.*

## Network overview

AIT has a wired and wireless network that covers all departments, labs, classrooms, the library, hostels, the canteen and open campus areas. A fibre backbone connects all departments, supported by about 14 km of structured cabling. Over 1,200 computers and devices are connected through more than 45 managed switches.

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Internet bandwidth | 1 Gbps total, from two providers (500 Mbps + 500 Mbps) |
| Connection to desktops | 100 Mbps or 1 Gbps |
| Server room | Air-conditioned, with UPS backup |
| Storage | 10 TB network storage on the intranet |
| Firewall | Gateway firewall with bandwidth control |

## Where internet is available (about 80 rooms)

36 classrooms, 40 labs, 2 computer centres, 1 research lab, 1 wireless network lab, 2 innovation labs, 3 seminar halls, an auditorium and a conference room. Staff rooms, the administration and accounts offices, the library, the reading hall, the placement office, hostels and the canteen also have access.

Two labs are kept open for exclusive internet use, each with more than 50 machines.

## Wi-Fi

- Wi-Fi covers the whole campus through controller-managed access points.
- **Registration:** register your device with its MAC address. You receive an **OTP by SMS** to verify. Each student can register up to 3 devices.
- Two levels of security: device registration and user login.
- Wi-Fi activity is monitored.

## Email and accounts

- Every student and faculty member gets an email account on the institute domain, using a cloud mail service. It can be used from anywhere.
- Accounts are created after admission is confirmed and disabled 6 months after graduation.

## Online examinations

A dedicated server runs the online exams and tests for the university and for the institute. Labs are reserved for exam days and are not available for general use.

## Security and acceptable use

- All computers sit behind the institute firewall and are protected by antivirus.
- **Do not share your login or password** with anyone, including friends or outsiders. This is a disciplinary offence.
- Viewing or downloading obscene or illegal material, and sending obscene messages using institute servers, is not allowed.
- Do not install pirated software on lab machines.

## Getting help

IT service desk: it-help@ait.example.edu. For Wi-Fi issues, send your device MAC address and registration number.
